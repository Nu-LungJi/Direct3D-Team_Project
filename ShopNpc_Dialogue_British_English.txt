GERBOLD OLLIVANDER — BRITISH ENGLISH DIALOGUE
==============================================

[FIRST VISIT]

Gerbold Ollivander: Ah, Professor Sharp. You have arrived.
Gerbold Ollivander: I have rather a lot on my hands... Do bear with me a moment!
Gerbold Ollivander: You have brought the money, I trust?

Choices:
  1) Yes
  2) No

Gerbold Ollivander: Ah, it seems you are a little short of money... I hear they are holding a small tournament at Hogwarts Castle. You had best make your way there!


[RETURNING WITH THE MONEY]

Gerbold Ollivander: Ah, you have managed to gather all the money!
Gerbold Ollivander: Right then—go on and choose yourself a wand!


[AFTER PURCHASING A WAND]

Gerbold Ollivander: Oh, what a splendid wand. Shall we learn a spell together whilst we are at it?


[AFTER THE SPELL LESSON]

Gerbold Ollivander: That is not a spell to be used carelessly. Do be careful!
